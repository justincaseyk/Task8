Insights from the report 
1.	Furniture has the highest individual sales.
2.	Technology (especially phones like Konftel 250 Conference Phone) also has high-value sales, While Furniture drives big-ticket sales, Office Supplies may contribute more in bulk.
3.	West (California, Washington) and South (Texas, Florida) appear frequently in high-value orders.
4.	Corporate clients in the West and South are high-value targets for premium products.
5.	Furniture has the highest upsell potential.
6.	December has high sales (e.g., $3,083.43 bookcase on 12/21/2016).
7.	Mid-year (June–September) also shows large orders.
8.	Year-end and mid-year may be peak buying seasons (budget cycles?).
